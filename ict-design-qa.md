**Visual direction**

ICT is the more contemporary and systems-oriented research preset. Source Sans 3 / Noto Sans SC provides precise headings and body copy, Source Serif 4 / Noto Serif SC is reserved for quotations, and JetBrains Mono supports code and tabular details. The official orbital emblem supplies the cyan and navy accents, but institutional identity is deliberately quieter than the research content.

**Research refinement**

- Reduced heading weight, scale, and tracking pressure while increasing body line height.
- Narrowed the cover brand field and softened its divider, watermark, title, subtitle, and author treatment.
- Reduced ordinary-slide emblem size and made the footer rule, page number, and chrome less prominent.
- Muted the section gradient and reduced its emblem, watermark, divider, and display weight.
- Replaced the former full-width Quote rule with a short `83.1875px × 1px` cyan-gray divider generated by `::before`.
- Softened table headers, callouts, TOC numbering, center headings, and statement accents.

**Findings**

No actionable P0, P1, or P2 findings remain.

- All ten slides report zero content-area overflow at 1960 × 1104.

- The Quote wrapper and Markdown child blockquote have no top or left borders; only the short 1px divider remains.
- Slides 2–10 have no frame-level top decoration. The cover retains only its intentional full-height vertical composition divider.
- The supplied SVGs remain source assets rather than being redrawn or approximated.
